# Scope

What you own and what you're trying to move. This is what Claude reasons about when you ask "what should I focus on" or when the weekly workflows prioritize.

- **What you own:** (products, areas, surfaces, workstreams)
- **What you're accountable for:** (the outcomes or metrics you're measured on)
- **This period's priorities:** (the 1 to 3 things that matter most right now)
- **Standing initiatives:** (longer-running things that stay on your radar even when quiet)
- **Explicitly not yours:** (things people might assume you own but you don't - useful for routing)

## Topics
As specific projects recur, give each its own file under `memory/topics/<topic>.md` and add a routing row in CLAUDE.md so it loads automatically.
